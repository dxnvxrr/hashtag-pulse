What we collect

  • A guest account. No email, phone number or real name. The game creates an account on first launch, tied to a random ID made on your device (we store only a one-way hash of it), the device model and the platform.
  • Gameplay. Your tap timings for each run, scores, Prestige, Precision rating, lifetime stats, daily streak, and challenge results. The server needs the tap timings to check every run is real.
  • Leaderboard profile. A generated name (like “Swift Comet 4821”), the region you pick or that we guess from your device’s time zone, and the time zone itself (your streak counts days where you live). We never look up your location.
  • Age. If you answer the age question, we keep only whether you are under 13 and whether you are 18 or older, not your birth year.
  • Security records. IP addresses in our security log, used to stop abuse and cheating.
  • Game analytics. Anonymous events such as loading time, crashes and difficulty. For players under 13 we accept only technical events.

Ads

The game is free and shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for extra lives. Ads come from Google (AdMob in the app, AdSense on the web). Google may use device identifiers to show and measure ads, and on iPhone only if you allow tracking. Players under 13, and anyone who hasn’t answered the age question, get non-personalised ads. See how Google uses information from sites and apps.

Prizes

If monthly skill prizes are offered where you live and you win, you verify your identity, age and country with our verification partner before payment. We keep only the partner’s reference, your country and the payment record.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data. We don’t ask for contacts, photos, location or a camera. Leaderboards show only your generated name, region and scores.

Deleting your data

Open Unlocks & Sound and tap Delete my data. Your account is removed from leaderboards at once and erased within 30 days; IP addresses in our security log are erased with it. You can also email us.

Children

The game is for everyone. If you tell us you are under 13 we turn off personalised ads and collect only technical analytics. Prizes are only for adults.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion. Email us and we will reply within 30 days.
